Operating principles

How I work

Architecture is a cost decision

Running ML on-device rather than in the cloud wasn't a technical preference. It holds infrastructure near zero as usage scales. Every significant technical choice has a line item attached to it.

Build what's differentiating, buy what's table stakes

Never build auth. Never build an annotation tool. Spend engineering time on the thing competitors can't copy.

Ship the piece that stands alone first

On a recent project, the consumer-facing product was viable independent of the R&D-dependent features. That sequencing de-risks everything downstream.

The hardest problems are at the seams

Most technology organizations fail translating between science, engineering, and executive teams, not writing code. I treat that translation as part of the job.
